EWAN HUMAN RESOURCES

Ewan Human Resources has entered a new partnership with Musaned, the Kingdom's leading platform for managing the recruitment of foreign labor. The partnership will give Ewan clients a more streamlined and transparent path to recruiting foreign workers, in compliance with Saudi labor laws.

Through Musaned, Ewan manages the full recruitment process — visa issuance, contracts and worker rights — helping ensure a smooth, legal process for both employers and employees, and giving families access to trusted, reliable domestic staff.

For more information, our customer service team is ready to help.
