EWAN HUMAN RESOURCES

Ewan Human Resources has opened several new branches across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, extending its reach to serve clients more directly. The company now operates branches in Riyadh, Al Kharj, Al Rass, Madinah, Taif, Jeddah, Abha, Dammam, Khobar, Al Ahsa, Qassim, Hail, Tabuk and Al Jouf.

The expansion is designed to put Ewan's full range of HR services within easier reach of clients across every region, meeting growing demand from businesses and individuals with the same commitment to innovative, professional service.

Branches welcome visitors Saturday to Thursday from 9am to 10pm, and Fridays from 5pm to 10pm. Ewan continues to focus on delivering strong service while contributing to the development of the Kingdom's workforce.

For more information, our customer service team is ready to help.
